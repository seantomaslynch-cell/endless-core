Privacy Policy — Endless Core

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Endless Core is a single-player drilling game. This policy explains what data the app does — and does not — collect.

No account, no server, no personal data collection

Endless Core does not require sign-in, does not have a server backend, and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you.

What is stored, and where

Your gameplay progress — banked gold, upgrade levels, high score, collected Relics, selected Drill Class, unlocked cosmetic trails, and a short local history of past runs — is saved only on your own device, using standard device storage. None of it is sent to us or to any third party.

Advertising and analytics

This version of the app does not include any advertising network and does not collect advertising identifiers or tracking data. It does not integrate any third-party analytics or crash-reporting service.

Web version

A separate browser-based build of this game is also available for the YouTube Playables platform. That build interacts with YouTube's own Playables SDK (for score submission and optional ads), which is governed by YouTube's own Privacy Policy. The iOS app you're using does not include that integration.

Children's privacy

Because Endless Core collects no personal information at all, this applies equally to users of any age, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated to reflect that.
