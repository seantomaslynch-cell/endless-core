Privacy Policy — Endless Core

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Endless Core is a single-player drilling game. This policy explains what data the app does — and does not — collect.

No account, no server

Endless Core does not require sign-in and does not have a server backend of its own. The only data collection in the app comes from the third-party advertising service described below.

What is stored, and where

Your gameplay progress — banked gold, upgrade levels, high score, collected Relics, selected Drill Class, unlocked cosmetic trails, and a short local history of past runs — is saved only on your own device, using standard device storage. None of it is sent to us or to any third party.

Advertising

Endless Core shows ads through Google AdMob to support free play: a periodic interstitial ad, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game benefit (reviving your drill, doubling your gold, or a temporary gold magnet). To do this, AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier and other technical/usage data, and may use it to show you more relevant ads, subject to the permission you grant when the app asks. You can decline tracking permission and still play the game and watch ads — declining just means the ads you see are less personalized. See Google's ad policy and how AdMob handles data for details. This app does not integrate any other third-party analytics or crash-reporting service.

Web version

A separate browser-based build of this game is also available for the YouTube Playables platform. That build interacts with YouTube's own Playables SDK (for score submission and optional ads), which is governed by YouTube's own Privacy Policy. The iOS app you're using does not include that integration.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children, and ad personalization is subject to the age and consent settings configured in AdMob for this app.

Changes to this policy

If this app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated to reflect that.
