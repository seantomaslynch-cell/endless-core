Support — Endless Core

Endless Core is an endless vertical drilling game — steer down through procedurally generated terrain, dodge stone, grab gold, and see how deep you can go.

Need help, found a bug, or have feedback?
Email lynch57@hotmail.com and include your device model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem — it makes it much faster to track down.

Frequently asked

How do I steer?
Hold the left or right half of the screen to steer the drill in that direction. Release to fall straight down.

How do I pause?
Tap the pause button in the top-right corner during a run.

Do I need an account?
No. There's no sign-in — your progress (gold, upgrades, Relics, high score) is saved automatically on your own device.

Are there ads?
A periodic ad may appear between runs, and you can optionally watch a rewarded ad to revive, double your gold, or activate a gold magnet — all of those are entirely your choice, never required to keep playing.
